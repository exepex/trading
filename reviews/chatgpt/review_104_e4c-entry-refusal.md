# Review 104 — E4c entry refusal
**Scope:** story/e4c-protective-leg-refusal @ 8e53630, response 121 and judge 120. **Overall: ACCEPT story direction, PARTIAL on representativeness.** Story-text review; code is not yet built. Evidence labels follow AGENTS.md.

1. **All legs or none — ACCEPT (reasoning only; strong evidence: E4c scope/AC).** A single snapshot cannot model reliable sequential fills or legging. Prechecking all opening sides and protective exit liquidity avoids knowingly manufacturing an immediate round trip. It selects for liquid contracts and thus estimates the performance of this *restricted execution policy*, not every strategy a real trader could implement. Report rejected opportunities as zeros in opportunity-level results, never erase them.

2. **Top-of-book size — PARTIAL (reasoning only).** For 65 units, 20 at the touch does not prove the other 45 cannot fill at a worse price; equally it does not prove they can. With only level-1 data, **refuse**, and record `insufficient_top_size` and required units privately. Do not invent deeper fills or assume 65 at the quoted price. Later compare observed fillability with full depth or broker execution evidence, and report refusal rate by structure/expiry.

3. **Spread-width threshold — ACCEPT deferral (reasoning only).** No defensible fixed percentage threshold is established for the actual contract, tenor and market. The touch-side net entry and approved maximum-loss/cost limit already bound modeled entry economics. Report median/p90 spread and rejected opportunities; pre-register a threshold only if A16 forward data supports it. A wide spread still creates exit risk; entry cap is not a substitute for monitoring.

4. **E8 R=5/30/60 — ACCEPT with test (strong evidence: E4c scope).** Apply the same check independently at each R snapshot. A trade refused at R=5 may pass at R=30; do not carry forward a quote or count multiple R simulations as independent trades. Ensure R=60 next-session gap uses the later market and revised risk check, not just old C4 approval.

5. **Public record and scoreboards — PARTIAL (strong evidence: E4c record specification).** Normal refusal emits no fills, but the **defensive unwind** may still publish both touch sides through opposite fill rows; E2b's aggregate/account leakage is separate. Test a forced post-check fill failure and scan all public outputs, alerts and derived scoreboards together. Refusal record must include opportunity ID, code, reason, snapshot hash, first failing leg and status without quote prices/sizes; scorecards count no-fill, refusal and opportunity cost. Never equate a refusal with a losing executed trade.
