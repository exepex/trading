# Review 103 — C5 risk buckets
**Scope:** story/c5-risk-buckets @ b929743; response 117, C5 story and judge 105. **Overall: PARTIAL; acceptable as inactive risk infrastructure, with a brake-authentication gate before counted paper.** Evidence labels follow AGENTS.md. Judge's 46 mutations are reported, not independently rerun.

1. **Paths around caps/brake — PARTIAL (strong evidence: C5 story AC3, AC5, AC8, AC13; some evidence: judge 105).** Same-session round trips count toward ₹50,000, dual-sided positions count full max loss in each bucket, unknown buckets fail closed, and C4 independently recomputes. A partial fill must be represented by its *largest actually carried* risk and its first-fill session, not merely the final open quantity; test fill→reduce→close, restart, and a journal replay with a partial leg. Unknown old session history must refuse new entries. No evidence here of a bypass in the specified C4-gated path, but don't assert every future caller is safe without an end-to-end test binding C4 PASS to the exact proposal and journal head.

2. **Bucket design — PARTIAL (reasoning only; strong evidence for arithmetic).** Full max loss in both direction and volatility buckets is conservative **overlap**, not additive portfolio loss: ₹10k in bullish and ₹10k in short-vol consumes ₹10k of each ₹30k limit, but only ₹10k of the ₹150k total open-risk cap. This is acceptable as a first screening taxonomy, not a statistical correlation model. Long calls/puts have convexity but are not pure long-vol bets across all strikes and horizons; a large IV crush can hurt. Test stress P&L and net Greeks across all positions before claiming diversification. NIFTY/BANKNIFTY are correlated even in separate groups: cross-index stress remains necessary.

3. **Orchestrator choices — ACCEPT provisionally (reasoning only).** Per-book ₹20 lakh, ₹120k brake and caps preserve the experimental books' independent accounting; don't pool them into one real-money budget. LC/LP in direction and long-vol buckets is a conservative proxy, not a guaranteed hedge. **Owner confirmation remains required** for both autonomous decisions, as response 117 explicitly says; absent confirmation, do not treat them as owner-approved binding policy.

4. **Signed restart — PARTIAL (strong evidence: C5 AC8).** A non-empty `entered_by` and reason is not a cryptographic signature: anyone with write access can forge a restart row. Adequate for a non-live, manually supervised *draft* paper account only if Git permissions and lane-1 review are enforced; **not adequate before counted autonomous paper decisions**. Require a separate owner-approved restart artifact with immutable commit/hash and verification that its author/approval is the owner, bound to book, brake episode, effective time and reason; reject copied, stale or future approvals. Tests: fake typed name, altered journal, duplicate restart and replay. No real order capability.
