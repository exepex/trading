## Four blocking cross-file issues

**B1 — Decision cutoff versus advocate issuance: CHANGE (strong evidence).** `04_layers_card_monitor_learning.md` §4.1 says market decision cutoff 15:00 and card delivery 15:08; `07_ai_team.md` §7.3 requires the advocate to read the card and issue objections *before the decision cutoff*. This cannot work if the card is built after 15:00. **Exact fix:** distinguish `market_data_cutoff=15:00`, `decision_finalized_at`, and `card_sent_at`. Freeze market inputs at 15:00; build a proposed decision, run code checks and advisory objections on that immutable pack, finalize before 15:08, then send. No later market data may enter. If AI times out, record `advocate_unavailable` and send with mandatory code checks, not a fictional objection. Test timing and timeouts.

**B2 — Empty shadow set is not statistical futility: CHANGE (strong evidence).** `08_budget_and_stop_rules.md` §8.2 says Test B is NO when no idea reached rung 2, while its own principle says underpowered is UNDECIDED and NO requires an upper confidence bound below a useful effect. No observations means no confidence bound. **Exact fix:** zero eligible registered/resolved shadows => `UNDECIDED/no_economic_test`, not NO. NO requires adequately powered registered tests whose upper bounds rule out the predeclared useful net. The owner may stop for cost/product reasons with no ideas, but that is a separate decision, not statistical rejection. Test the A/B decision table for zero trials, missing data and void outcomes.

**B3 — QLIKE zero outcome and flooring: CHANGE (strong mathematical evidence).** `05_volatility_research.md` §5.4 uses `RV/f - ln(RV/f) - 1`; this is undefined at RV=0. Flooring observed RV to 1% of trailing median makes it computable but **changes the measured target** and, according to response 96, affects 132/2174 (6.1%) one-day close-proxy outcomes. **Exact fix:** consider the equivalent-for-positive-RV QLIKE ranking `log(f) + RV/f`, which is defined at RV=0 for f>0 and avoids changing observations. Freeze this as primary unless a justified observed-RV floor is deliberately selected and sensitivity reported. Tests must cover zero, near-zero, missing and two forecast rankings; never select the formulation after viewing scores.

**B4 — Forecast-first rule blocks useful forward evidence: CHANGE (strong evidence on dependencies; reasoning only on remedy).** `01_overview.md` §§1.3/1.7 and `03_layers_factory_desk.md` G3 require a forecast to pass before V-ideas advance, while §5.5 projects 15 months–5 years for some daily tests and far longer for 20-day questions. This needlessly blocks the collection of **costed forward shadow decisions**. **Exact fix:** permit pre-registered, cost-screened `exploratory_shadow` collection in parallel with forecast evaluation, with no claim of promotion or paper eligibility until independent forecast/economic gates pass. Preserve the owner's no-real-order and paper-evidence limits. Add this distinction to R3 and REPLAN.

## Full file-by-file review

Say “two different studies on related historical data.” The 1,200–2,000-trade calculation assumes fixed SD and approximately independent trades; label it illustrative, not universal. Fix B4.

### 02_layers_data_forecast_weather.md — CHANGE
**AGREE (reasoning only):** eight layers, point-in-time provenance and scoring weather are coherent. **CHANGE (strong evidence):** 09:15–15:30 inclusive at 15-minute steps gives 26 snapshots but 25 intraday intervals; specify overnight-to-open return and missing-interval rules. Quotes are not necessarily traded prices: define midpoint/last and freshness, and label quote-based realised-variance proxy honestly. US market closes, FRED publication and later revisions need separate first-seen timestamps. **CHANGE (reasoning only):** RED 2.5x/AMBER 1.5x plus absolute floors and a 15% frequency review are unvalidated; frequency alarm cannot override the owner's RED entry block. Record matched shadow costs of blocked trades.

### 03_layers_factory_desk.md — CHANGE
**AGREE (reasoning only):** feasibility wall, registered screened-out ideas, Rs20 lakh account and Rs10k per-trade cap. **CHANGE (strong evidence):** a gross “conservative bound” must define its probability/tail assumptions; the 2x warning cannot be a profit test. Owner-timing R=60 on a 15:08 card implies next-session entry around 10:15, with gaps and many missed ranges; no synthetic fill when no valid quote. Three R variants are correlated outcomes, not independent trials. **CHANGE (economic reasoning):** report full-account return including idle cash against full-account index/cash benchmarks, not just “per rupee margin tied” versus an index fund; match horizon, fees and taxes. 31.2% is a scenario, not verified personal tax. Fix B4.

### 04_layers_card_monitor_learning.md — CHANGE
**AGREE (reasoning only):** immutable daily cards, NO TRADE, alerts and why-wrong review. **CHANGE (strong evidence):** fix B1. ### 05_volatility_research.md — CHANGE
**AGREE (strong evidence on distinctions):** volatility is more forecastable than direction in many regimes, but forecast skill is not trade profit. V5 as a terminal intrinsic-payoff probability, with separately measured shadow P&L, is acceptable; a profit target need not replace it. **CHANGE:** fix B3; G6 must freeze exact null-centred one-sided block-bootstrap p-value, seed, block lengths, missingness, Holm family and zero-case QLIKE. **CHANGE (strong evidence):** V4's conversion between calendar IV and session RV needs a dimensionally checked golden example (annualised volatility versus integrated variance). Show raw implied, HAR and EWMA. V5's baseline is a probability, never IV squared. The 316-outcome power table assumes approximately independent loss differences; 60-day projections cannot authorize threshold changes.

### 06_risk_and_alerts.md — CHANGE
**AGREE (reasoning only):** bucket limits and stress aggregation are appropriate. **CHANGE (strong evidence):** distinguish maximum terminal payoff loss, legging/execution loss, stressed mark loss and realised loss. A Rs10k terminal-payoff cap does not universally bound all execution/settlement outcomes. Freeze margin-source age, bucket mapping, concentration and missing-margin fail-closed behavior. Define whether silent monitoring blocks new entries and how the owner-unavailable fallback works; never assume phone response within a guaranteed time.

### 07_ai_team.md — CHANGE
**AGREE (reasoning only):** advisory AI objections, mandatory code vetoes, immutable provenance and a baseline fallback. **CHANGE (strong evidence):** fix B1. Predeclare a paired real-vs-paper net/slippage test and drawdown stop. Verify chosen structure actually fits the smaller Rs4k–5k real-test cap and verify current SEBI/tax requirements at deployment.
