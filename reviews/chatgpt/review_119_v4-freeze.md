## 4. Late, missed and late-starting forecasts — CHANGE (strong evidence for G9 behavior; reasoning only for fairness remedy)

**ACCEPT** G9's observed-only scoring, >10% overall and >10% missed 2%-move day thresholds, no imputation, and operations-undecided rather than false futility. `fno/forecast_v4/misses.py:miss_report` uses `first = start or first non-null forecast`; when a slot is registered with a known start, callers must supply that start so a later outage cannot reset its miss clock.

**Remaining real fairness loophole:** the plan permits AI slots to register/start after the freeze and counts **no misses before their first live forecast**. This is legitimate for a genuinely new slot, but an existing forecaster could delay its first submission until a favourable regime and avoid the earlier difficult days. It can change a claim or the stop decision. **Minimal fix:** require the registration ledger to name a prospective `effective_start` before the first eligible session; from that date every baseline-observable day counts as observed or missed, even if the first actual forecast arrives later. A genuinely not-yet-built slot remains `not_started` and ineligible for claims until its registration; no retroactive cherry-picking or backdating. Freeze the start rule, not all future registrations. Test a registered AI slot missing its first three baseline-observable days and submitting on day four: three misses must count. This is a small G8 adapter rule, not a new service.
