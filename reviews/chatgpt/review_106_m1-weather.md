# Review 106 — M1 weather forecaster
**Scope:** story/m1-weather-forecaster @ 438b8bc, response 130, judge 129. **Overall: PARTIAL; model is suitable as an exploratory forecaster, not yet a proven entry veto.** Evidence labels follow AGENTS.md; figures are reported from judge/builder, not independently recomputed.

1. **Replace v0 operative colour — PARTIAL (some evidence).** v0 RED on 26% of observed weeks exceeds the owner's 15% alarm-review threshold; the VIX model is simpler and has a measured backfill. But **RED share is not predictive accuracy**, and neither model has established forward calibration or economic benefit. Accept `weather_vix_v1` as the explicitly **unproven** operative research colour only under the owner's confirmation of autonomous decision 71. Before counted trading, require a recorded decision that defines how the RED/missing gate operates while evidence is absent. G8 must score v0, VIX and base rate on the same resolved opportunities; do not silently switch on the basis of one favourable period.

2. **VIX-only empirical tails — ACCEPT as first baseline (reasoning only).** Scaling historic returns by lagged VIX is point-in-time and avoids fitted parameters; it preserves observed clustering for the 5-day target. But VIX prices risk-neutral variance with premium and may under/overpredict physical tail risk. Keep scheduled RBI/Fed events, US gaps, expiry and positioning as **descriptive drivers** until forward tests justify adding them. Macro/flow/equity/news analysts should inspect false negatives, not hand-edit the model after seeing outcomes.

3. **Colour thresholds — PARTIAL (some evidence).** The owner's fixed floors/multiples are reproducible: RED if p1 >= max(10%,2.5×b1) or p5 >= max(35%,2.5×b5); AMBER if p1 >= max(6%,1.5×b1) or p5 >= max(20%,1.5×b5). Reported RED shares range from 34% in 2020 to 0% in 2021/2023, reflecting strong regime sensitivity. The 15% rolling frequency is a **review trigger**, not a reason to override RED. It may be too quiet in calm years or block too often in crashes; this is not yet validated safety. Publish blocked opportunities, subsequent 2%-day rate and matched shadow net on both sides of the gate.

4. **Staleness and excessive blocking — PARTIAL (strong evidence for story rules; reasoning only for future behavior).** Missing probabilities/base rate blocks entries, but delayed daily closes, stale VIX, missing A17b private cues and calendar mistakes have different effects. US cue failure is only a missing descriptive driver and **must not block** the forecast; missing current-required VIX/history does. Require field-level `as_of`/coverage-through timestamps, a hard previous-session completeness gate, idempotent capture before 15:00 and explicit `missing_weather` status. A delayed capture should not retrospectively unblock a trade. Test special sessions, event announcement cutoff, stale VIX, empty same-colour v0 cohort, 250-record rolling share and 2020-style clustered extremes.
