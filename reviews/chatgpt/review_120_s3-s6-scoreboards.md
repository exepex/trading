# Review 120 — S3 and S6 scoreboards (quality rule 9)

**Target:** `story/s6-scorecard-measures`, S3/S6 code through `f6d0d379`; Response 193, stories, builder responses 179/180, judges 168/191/158/196, S1 protocol and scoring implementation. **Verdict: PARTIAL / CHANGE before S1 freeze.** I inspected code and the judges' claims; I did not independently run the reported test suites. Confidence labels follow AGENTS.md. The system is paper-only.

## 1. Take / skip / not observed — PARTIAL (strong evidence)

`matched_actions.py:_not_chosen` correctly treats `margin_room`, `bucket_cap`, `drawdown_brake`, `max_open_trades` and similar **account** constraints as hypothetical takes for a *standardised idea-quality* scoreboard. A book with ₹0 remaining margin should not have its idea marked bad merely because its account was full. C4 hard-invalid and `max_loss_per_trade` are correctly skips for a strategy required to obey hard validity and its per-trade loss ceiling. **Qualification:** a risk-manager-driven decision to abstain for a market reason must remain a genuine skip, not be mapped to an account refusal merely because the risk manager used a generic `inputs` rule. `ACCOUNT_LIMIT_RULES` includes `inputs` for an already-held same trade; pin this meaning in a fixture and list each take's original refusal reason. Scoreboard A must be labelled *hypothetical one-lot idea quality*, never actual executable book return.

## 2. Standard unit — PARTIAL (strong evidence)

`standard_unit.py:StandardUnit.value` correctly requires all opening legs fillable at the same snapshot; C1 charges, B2 max loss and E7 settlement are used. But `_exit` iterates over each remaining leg on each session from the prescribed exit until the report date and removes legs **independently** as quotes permit. This can leave a partly hedged vertical open while its other leg exits later. That is not the same economic policy as the book's own E4/E8 exits, and may either flatter or punish one book depending on strike liquidity. It is acceptable as an explicitly *common benchmark*, but the head-to-head claim must not be described as a comparison of each book's executable strategy P&L. **Required diagnostic before freeze:** report the count of staggered-leg exits and the resulting holding days; if nonzero in normal data, add a simple same-snapshot all-leg exit sensitivity alongside the primary metric (do not change the frozen primary after results). A wide-spread book already pays the touch and may not fill; show no-fill share by book. A book-specific faster exit is intentionally excluded from this common time-exit estimand.

## 3. Cut at first open take — PARTIAL (strong evidence)

`matched.py:scored_days` **breaks** at the first decision day containing an OPEN take. If an older position remains open while newer takes have closed, the code discards all newer days. At monthly expiry this is a real temporary condition, not an adversarial scenario. Example: an entry on day 1 remains open through day 20, while entries on days 2–19 close profitably or poorly; until day 1 resolves, the reported series stops before day 1. This prevents treating open P&L as zero, but can delay visibility of subsequent bad decisions.

**Simplest fix:** keep the confirmatory contiguous-prefix rule (no provisional profit claims), but show **after-cut completed trades and their provisional net**, plus the count/age of unresolved takes, as a clearly non-claim diagnostic. If the cut persists past the contract's expiry plus normal settlement availability, label the book `incomplete` and block any claim; do not treat later trades as zero or silently reset the sample start. This changes what the owner sees about possible losses without adding a second inferential engine.

## 4. Pairs and Holm — PARTIAL (strong evidence)

`matched.py:pair_series` compares daily **one-lot rupees** on common days with risk=1. This answers “which book's standard one-lot ideas made more rupees?”, not “which used risk more efficiently?”. Because B2 max loss differs by strike/structure, a high-risk book can win in rupees while losing on net per unit of risk. Example: A earns ₹400 on ₹8,000 max loss (5%); B earns ₹300 on ₹2,000 (15%). Rupee head-to-head picks A. **Keep the primary rupee comparison if that is the registered estimand**, but show each book's net/risk and risk gap adjacent to the pair verdict; do not call a rupee win better risk-adjusted performance.

`matched_board.py:_apply_holm` includes only claims with `sample_met` and available p-values. That is not automatically invalid if the **family is prospectively fixed and missing claims cannot be selectively dropped**. But the code uses `permutations(self.books,2)`, so A>B and B>A both enter alongside per-book claims; eligibility changes the Holm denominator over time. Before S1 freeze, explicitly freeze the family of book claims and directional pairs, with unavailable members recorded as p=1 for the scheduled look (rather than silently reducing m). This prevents a later optional-start/late-resolution member from giving the others a looser test. Test a three-book review with one ineligible pair; its presence must not improve the others' multiplicity threshold. A fixed family is the minimal change; no new Holm implementation.

## 5. Do-nothing and tax — PARTIAL (strong evidence for code definitions; reasoning only on fiscal assumptions)

The index proxy (NIFTY price-close change + trailing dividend yield/365 −0.05%/year) is a defensible *approximate total return*, not an investable fund's actual NAV. The yield is trailing, avoiding future dividends, but the proxy omits tracking error, fund transaction costs and cash-flow timing. View (a), per rupee-day **actually tied**, is an appropriate opportunity-cost comparison for RM1 because idle capital should not be treated as risk deployed. View (b), whole-account with idle cash, is essential context for whether the household would have been better off doing nothing; report both, and do not claim a view-(a) pass guarantees whole-account outperformance.

**Cash-rate recommendation: 5.5% annual pre-tax as a provisional scenario, then freeze the contemporaneous RBI policy repo rate, not a guessed future yield** (reasoning only). The existing `(set at the freeze)` guard in `fno/evaluation/completeness.py` correctly prevents an accidental 5.5% default from becoming a binding gate. If the owner prefers an *investable* cash benchmark, use the observed 91-day Treasury-bill yield on the freeze date instead; don't substitute a liquid fund's hypothetical yield without an actual dated series.

**Tax caution:** 31.2% F&O and cash are *owner-specific marginal-rate assumptions*, not universal FY2026–27 rates; losses and tax timing can matter. Equity mutual-fund STCG **20% plus applicable cess = 20.8%** is plausible under the stated assumption. LTCG **12.5% plus 4% cess = 13%**, with the ₹1.25 lakh annual exemption, is plausible for qualifying equity funds, but surcharge, residential status, holding period and the exact fund type must be checked before RM1. The benchmark must not be labelled a tax determination. **Judge 196 note 1:** `matched_measures.py` truncates capital-days when a closed take exits beyond the scored prefix and emits `capital_cut`; a label alone is **not sufficient for a binding comparison**. A's view (a) is descriptive and must show `not comparable` if `capital_cut` is nonzero; B's RM1 gate must use the complete capital path, not A's truncated one.

## 6. t-check — ACCEPT (strong evidence)

`t_check.py:t_bound` uses `z_b=(net_b−R×risk_b)/mean(risk_b)`, which is the first-order linearisation for a ratio estimator with unequal block risk. It uses whole 20-session blocks and a t critical value with k−1 degrees of freedom; above df=30 it conservatively retains t30. At k=2 the estimate is extremely noisy, so a **warning-only** result is appropriate. Do not make it a second gate without an error-rate/power study.

## 7. Random control — PARTIAL (strong evidence)

`random_control.py:_twin` draws direction and strike, then **rejects and redraws** if the result is unquotable or violates max-loss/bucket caps. This measures selection relative to **feasible** random alternatives, not uniform random alternatives. That is a reasonable descriptive control, but directional feasibility is asymmetric: a bullish mirror may be rejected much more often in one regime. With 20 redraws the accepted direction distribution can depart far from 50/50, and the control can look more/less favourable. **Minimal fix:** report accepted twin direction share and rejection reasons (quote, per-trade cap, bucket cap), and explicitly label the null as `feasible-random selection on the same dates`. Keep >10% missing twins as not computable. Do not use it as an inferential gate.

## 8. Missed runs — PARTIAL (strong evidence)

`matched_board.py:_misses` excludes `NO_SNAPSHOT`, `NO_PACK`, `UNSCORED` and `F7_NOT_BUILT` from the 10% book-specific miss denominator. Common no-snapshot/no-pack days should not penalise one model, but a *book-specific* D6 unscored day can remove a bad session. F7-not-built is a pre-start limitation, not evidence of skill. Keep common system outages separate; if `UNSCORED` is caused by one book's invalid version/inputs rather than a system-wide freeze, count it as an operational miss for that book after its registered start. **Concrete test:** one book is D6-unscored on a valid common-pack day while the other two submit; it must not improve that book's miss rate. `CommittedRuns.missed_runs` also marks an exits step with `reviewed:false` but no `error`/`waiting` as reviewed; make `_reviewed` require `outcome.get("reviewed") is True` so a failed exit review cannot disappear from S6's operational report. That failure can affect the owner's risk/stop assessment.

## 9. Freeze changes and cost — PARTIAL / CHANGE

**Before S1 freeze, fix only result-affecting contracts:** (i) fixed Holm family with unavailable members p=1; (ii) D6 book-specific unscored days in the miss cap; (iii) `_reviewed` false-state reporting. These are small changes/tests to existing modules. Keep the standard unit and contiguous prefix as registered but disclose staggered exits, open-tail provisional performance and capital-cut non-comparability; these are reporting clarifications, not a new execution system. Verify the actual cash rate at freeze and pin the tax scenario; no additional random-control model or new statistical service.

## Financial-role challenge

**Mathematician/statistician/quant modeller:** different denominators, fixed multiplicity family, truncated series and bootstrap assumptions can reverse claims. **F&O/options/volatility trader:** liquidity, Greeks, overnight gaps and independent leg exits alter realised economics. **Risk/portfolio/head of desk:** a false miss count or failed exit report can hide operational risk; B must govern RM1, not A's diagnostic. **Market research/macro/derivatives flows/equity research/investment banking:** market events correlate books; common dates and honest missingness are essential. **Execution/microstructure:** touch-side depth and staggered exits are not a guarantee of executable spread fills. **Regulation:** current NSE lot/expiry and applicable SEBI rules must be checked before real use. **Behavioural:** don't present filled-only winners or selected controls as proof. **Tax/cost accounting:** flat charges, capital-days and tax status can dominate small net edges. **Data engineer/red team:** plant ineligible pair, one-book D6 unscored, `reviewed:false`, staggered exit and capital cut.

**Final verdict: PARTIAL / CHANGES REQUIRED before S1 freeze.** The S3/S6 architecture is useful, but the fixed claim family and two operational accounting cases can affect a pass or stop. No orders are authorized.
