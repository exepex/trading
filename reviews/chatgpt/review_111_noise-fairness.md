## Response 140 — three questions

**1. Observed forecast days, miss cap, 2%-day sub-cap, Test A — PARTIAL (strong evidence on the false-futility mechanism; reasoning only on cap).** Observed-only scoring is necessary: imputing a baseline forecast makes paired difference zero but the futility calculation adds a positive minimum-useful margin, so a model that never forecasts can be called futile. A 10% miss cap per question/slot is a reasonable *proposed* operating threshold, not a measured optimal value. A sub-cap for missing 2%-move days is justified because a slot could miss every rare event yet pass the overall cap. **Exact recommendation:** cap missed 2%-move opportunities at **10% of baseline-observable 2%-move days**, separately for each question/slot/look, with the count printed; when there are fewer than ten such days, permit **zero** misses to avoid rounding a single miss away. Baseline-missing days are `input_failure`, counted separately and must not improve eligibility. Freeze both caps before v4 scoring. For Test A, **keep an operations-undecided member from proving overall NO**; otherwise the stop rule silently changes its hypothesis family. Owner may stop the failed slot/project for operational reasons separately. An outage must not manufacture statistical futility.

**2. Measures — ACCEPT with substitution (reasoning only).** Keep net per rupee-day risk as headline, max drawdown/worst day, after-cost/tax do-nothing comparison and seeded random controls. Replace “largest loss as share of total gains” with **net without the single best trade and profit factor** (gross gains / absolute gross losses; undefined if no losses). These better expose dependence on one lucky outcome; print number of trades and total gains/losses so a zero denominator is not disguised. Random controls must share dates, opportunity universe, entry delays, structure eligibility, risk caps, fills and charges; otherwise a percentile measures a different experiment. The do-nothing comparison is supporting for S1 and a separate **owner-approved real-money gate**, not a new significance claim. Tax assumptions require verification when deployed.

**3. Stock recording window — PARTIAL (strong evidence on rate arithmetic; reasoning only on timing).** About 8,000 quote calls / 5 calls per second = **1,600 seconds = 26m40s**, before overhead. A 15:13–15:17 pause alone is insufficient to ensure E3/A16 gets its 15:15 quotes when independent processes share one Groww key; jobs may already be in flight or run slower. **Recommendation:** NIFTY commit/push first, other indices next; schedule stock recording **after 15:17** rather than merely pausing in mid-run, and hard-stop by 15:30. At 5/s, that yields at most 13 minutes × 5 × 60 = 3,900 calls, so **full 8,000-contract coverage is impossible in that window**. Therefore either accept a deterministic, predeclared subset with completeness reported, or move stock recording to a separate later market session/window where quotes are meaningful; never claim complete daily coverage. A shared cross-process pacer is overbuilt now. Measure combined request rates and fail/skip when the desk needs the key.

## Missed risks and role cross-check
