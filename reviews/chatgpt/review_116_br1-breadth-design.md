## Q1 — Corporate-action rule: PARTIAL (strong evidence for named cases, some evidence for generalisation)

**ACCEPT** the strike-continuity rule as the *primary data-native screen*: `sizing.py:adjusted` compares strike sets for the first common expiry and excludes a forecast date when less than half of its listed strikes survive to the next session. It detects the measured KOTAKBANK split, TATAMOTORS demerger, TRENT and VEDL adjustments (0% retained), while retaining KAYNES, POLICYBZR and the 27 election-crash names (100%). Judge 165 independently checked all 64 remaining >=15% moves against same-expiry futures. This is meaningful real-data validation. The observed cost is 116 excluded name-days, 27 with <1% spot moves; 259 non-adjusted dividend ex-days remain in the measured sample, with the judge bounding the overall Brier distortion at <0.2% of baseline Brier.

**Exact BR1a condition:** treat the rule as an **outcome-side eligibility filter**, not an input available at forecast time: whether strikes survive on t+1 is not known at t. Exclude consistently for every family and baseline, disclose the excluded count and NSE-list disagreements, and do not allow the eventual t+1 adjustment flag into the forecast's features. For dividends below the adjustment threshold, accept the measured residual distortion and disclose it rather than adding an unproven adjustment engine. The NSE list archived only from Oct 2026 cannot serve as the complete historical primary source. **Known limit:** an adjustment that preserves >50% of strikes can escape this screen; no such missed >=15% event was observed in the checked period.

## Q2 — Effective breadth and headline: PARTIAL (strong evidence for arithmetic; reasoning only for representativeness)

**ACCEPT** the cal60-vs-1.15×cal60 tilt as a conservative **scenario**, not a validated stand-in for an AI forecaster. The committed 631-scored-day report gives N=138, rho=0.105 and `138/(1+137×0.105)=8.98` effective bets. The top 30 give `30/(1+29×0.134)=6.14`; four indices give `4/(1+3×0.517)=1.57`. The same data show 41.5 effective bets for cal60-vs-trail60: correlation belongs to the **error-difference pair**, not to the universe. A uniform 15% volatility tilt resembles a market-wide regime bias; a realistic AI can also make name-specific earnings/flow errors. The tilt is one measured stress scenario, not a statistically established lower bound or universal forecast-error correlation.

**Recommendation:** headline the measured **range 6–42** and use **N_eff≈9** only as the predeclared common-tilt planning case. After the first prospective AI observations, report its actual daily cross-sectional score-difference SD and correlation without changing the frozen look dates. Do not promise that a different AI architecture has nine independent bets.

## Q3 — Primary baseline: PARTIAL (strong evidence for observed disagreement; reasoning only for claim policy)

**ACCEPT** selecting the lowest exploratory mean Brier among `market`, `trail60`, `cal60` on the predeclared 2019–2025 exploratory data, before freeze. This is materially better than asserting `cal60` is strongest: `cal60 vs trail60` has t=+1.65 on all 2024–26 names, t=−1.64 on Judge G's adjusted window, and t=+3.31 on the top 30. Simple baselines differ by as much as the proposed 2–3% edge.

**CHANGE the meaning of “must not lose to the other two (reported, not tested).”** An untested point-estimate requirement can be a conservative veto, but it **cannot support a claim of superiority to all three**. Fix this exact rule before freeze: the only confirmatory claim is **“beats the single preselected primary baseline”**; for the other two report paired effects and uncertainty and label them descriptive. If the owner instead wants to say **“beats all three baselines,”** make all three predeclared comparisons binding with multiplicity control (or an explicitly justified intersection-union test), and re-estimate power for that family. **Recommended simple option:** one confirmatory primary and descriptive alternatives; never advertise a primary-only pass as beating every simple baseline. A descriptive negative comparison may be disclosed as a caution but not silently recast as statistically established inferiority.

## Q4 — Holm, 5-session blocks and futility: PARTIAL — real pre-freeze statistical gap (strong evidence on code; some evidence on dependence)

**ACCEPT** collapsing all names on a date into one cross-sectional mean before temporal testing. That is already a **day-clustered observation**; a second cross-name independence assumption is not made by `confirmatory_test`. The reported daily lag-1 correlation reaches 0.26 in 2024, so five-session blocks are plausible for planning but do not prove correct false-positive rates. **Do not import** v4's NIFTY-specific calibrated thresholds or NIFTY 2%-day gates.

**CHANGE before a claim-rule freeze:** `fno/forecast_v4/confirmatory.py:confirmatory_test` computes `max(batch_means_p, studentized_bootstrap_p)`, but its p-value calibration was not demonstrated on BR1's **daily cross-sectional Brier differences**. The parent v4 protocol explicitly abandoned an uncalibrated test after observed null rejection inflation; BR1's plain test cannot be called 2%-per-look family-wise valid merely because it uses the same helper. BR1a must perform one **pre-registered empirical null/size check** on the available 2019–2025 daily differences, with the actual 5-session blocks, minimum 12 blocks, K=3, both looks and the selected 2019–2025 worst regime. Include both directions, and measure false **claim and futility** near the minimum useful effect. If rates exceed their declared limits, use a fixed more conservative calibrated threshold or postpone claims. This is a small research check on an already-used dataset, not a new service or trading feature. Do not tune a threshold using post-freeze outcomes. **A crash-day gate is not automatically required**; add one only if the actual BR1 regime study demonstrates a failure.

**Futility:** the planned plain one-sided p_e<=0.025 at look 1 needs the same boundary-null validation; otherwise a “no skill” verdict may be unreliable and affect the stop decision.

## Q5 — Months and K=3: PARTIAL (strong evidence for arithmetic; reasoning only for true future skill)

**ACCEPT K=3**, T1 EOD, one registered rules/hybrid/AI member each; T2, direction and 14:30 remain descriptive. The committed output gives 2% in **17.2 months**, 3% in **7.6 months** across 138 names; 2024's worst-year 2% figure **21.5 months**; top-30 AI subset **22.0 months** for 2%, and indices **81.2 months**. `sizing.py:months` uses `(z_(1−0.02/K)+z_0.8)^2 × SD² × (1+lag1)/(1−lag1) / (skill×baseline_Brier)^2 /21`, matching the report's planning framework. K=12 changes 17.2 to 22.3 months, about **30% longer**. These are **normal-approximation power projections**, not a guarantee of passing by those dates; the minimum 12 five-day blocks, registration/start delays, missing names and regime restrictions can only extend actual time.

**Do not tell the owner that 2–3% skill is empirically demonstrated or “realistic” as a known effect.** No prospective AI/Hybrid evidence exists. Say **“2% and 3% are hypothetical effect sizes used for power planning; whether any forecaster beats the frozen strongest baseline by that amount is unknown.”** The 1.15 tilt is itself not evidence of a beneficial model: the full-window `cal60 vs tilt` observed mean difference is **−0.0063**, t=−8.06, meaning the tilt **lost** on that history. This is a particularly important counterexample to treating the tilt as a plausible successful edge. A genuine 2% edge might never appear, so stop criteria and opportunity costs must remain explicit.

## Financial-role review and decisions

**Mathematician:** N_eff algebra is correct for a measured pair, not a property of all forecasts. **Statistician / quant researcher / quantitative modeller:** temporal size, primary-baseline selection and effect-size uncertainty dominate whether a “proof” is credible. **F&O trader / options strategist / volatility trader:** T1 is a frequent volatility-event prediction, not a tradable payoff; option premium, skew and after-cost spreads still decide profit. **Risk manager / portfolio manager / head of desk:** a false claim can trigger capital allocation; false futility can end research; keep both gates conservative. **Market research / macro / derivatives flow / equity research / investment banking:** correlated election/earnings/RBI/Fed shocks can reduce true breadth, and corporate actions can create fake crashes. **Execution/microstructure:** EOD bhavcopy closes are not executable bid/ask; forward shadow fills are separate. **Regulation:** no live orders and NSE contract rules remain applicable. **Behavioural:** resist calling 7.6 months a deadline for success or selecting only the winning subset. **Tax/cost accountant:** Brier skill is not after-tax F&O profit. **Data/backtesting engineer / red team:** verify outcome-side adjustment, missing-session universe and null calibration on the actual daily series.

**Final decision: PARTIAL.** Accept BR1 as an exploratory breadth-track direction and K=3 design. Before **confirmatory freeze**, (1) state exactly what a primary-only baseline pass claims, and (2) validate the BR1-specific claim/futility false-decision rates. No further corporate-action adjustment engine, crash gate or parallel scoring framework is justified without real evidence. This review does not approve trading or assert forecast skill.
