## 1. Why a rewrite: the owner's correction

The owner: *"the design is about a team of experts in different fields working together, to find the most profitable
trades, and winning against the odds. That is the design that we need to work upon ... we still did not close the
final design ... why should we move to next step until we fix the core step."*

**I agree, and the fault is shared.** Version 1 and your review 9 both spent most of their words on controls (judge,
ledger, timing, accounting). Those are needed and are kept, but neither said how a team of experts actually
**finds** winning trades. Version 2 starts there:

1. **The odds** (section 1): SEBI data, 88-93% of individuals lose every year, 93% only buy options, money flows to
   algorithmic desks. So we win only by standing on the side that is paid, at a horizon the crowd and the fast desks
   leave alone. First test of every trade: **who pays us, and why will they keep paying?**
2. **The edge map** (section 2): eight edge families (volatility premium, conditional volatility, event premium,
   term structure, skew, conditional direction, stock vs index, cost advantage), each with its payer, owning roles,
   data and current evidence, plus what is **not** ours (speed, expiry-day games, closing auction, lottery buying).
3. **How the team works** (section 3): every role owns edge families and writes finding cards; a trade thesis needs
   seven parts from different roles (payer, signal, context, structure, price, survival, attack), so no single role
   can make a trade; the meeting has independent findings, compulsory cross-examination, and disagreements settled
   by a test agreed before it runs, not by votes; each role has a scorecard of added net money.
