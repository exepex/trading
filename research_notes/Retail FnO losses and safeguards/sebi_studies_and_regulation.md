# SEBI studies of retail F&O losses and the regulatory changes that followed (India, 2023-2026)

Note on sourcing: SEBI's own study pages (sebi.gov.in) were reachable but the fetched HTML gave only the title and date, not the PDF text. Most figures below therefore come from press or broker write-ups that quote the studies. Where only secondary sources exist, this is flagged. Business Standard pages returned HTTP 403, so their figures are taken from search snippets.

## 1. SEBI study of January 2023 (FY22 data)

### Takeaway
On 25 Jan 2023 SEBI reported that 89% of individual equity F&O traders lost money in FY22, with an average loss of Rs 1.1 lakh. Loss-makers paid a further 28% of their net trading loss in transaction costs. The number of traders rose more than 500% in three years, driven by people under 30.

### Cited Findings
- Title: "Study - Analysis of Profit and Loss of Individual Traders dealing in Equity F&O Segment". Published 25 Jan 2023 — [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/research/jan-2023/study-analysis-of-profit-and-loss-of-individual-traders-dealing-in-equity-fando-segment_67525.html)
- Sample: individual clients of the top 10 brokers, who made up 67% of individual F&O turnover in FY22. The study covered FY19-FY22 — [Business Standard (search snippet)](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/markets/sebi-study-suggests-89-retail-traders-in-equity-f-o-suffered-losses-123012501466_1.html)
- Losses and profits in FY22:
  - 89% of individual traders lost money, with an average loss of Rs 1.1 lakh.
  - 11% made a profit, averaging Rs 1.5 lakh.
  - Among "active" traders, 90% lost money (average loss Rs 1.25 lakh) and 10% made a profit (average Rs 1.9 lakh).
  - Source: [Business Today, 25 Jan 2023](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/markets/stocks/story/loss-makers-of-dalal-street-9-out-of-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-in-fy22-says-sebi-367628-2023-01-25)
- Transaction costs:
  - Loss-makers spent an extra 28% of their net trading loss on transaction costs.
  - Traders with a net profit spent 15% to 50% of that profit on transaction costs.
  - Non-active profit-makers spent 6%.
  - Source: [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/markets/stocks/story/loss-makers-of-dalal-street-9-out-of-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-in-fy22-says-sebi-367628-2023-01-25)
- Number of traders: unique individual traders at the top 10 brokers rose from 7.1 lakh in FY19 to 45.2 lakh in FY22, an increase of more than 500% — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/markets/stocks/story/loss-makers-of-dalal-street-9-out-of-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-in-fy22-says-sebi-367628-2023-01-25)
- Age and gender:
  - The 20-30 age group's share of traders rose from 11% in FY19 to 36% in FY22.
  - The 30-40 group was the largest, at 39% in FY22.
  - More than 80% of traders were male.
  - Source: [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/markets/stocks/story/loss-makers-of-dalal-street-9-out-of-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-in-fy22-says-sebi-367628-2023-01-25)
- Follow-up: SEBI said it would issue guidelines on additional risk disclosures by brokers and exchanges. These became the broker-login risk disclosure, "9 out of 10 individual traders ... incurred net losses" — [NSDL risk disclosure page](https://nsdl.co.in/publications/risk_Disclosure.php); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/markets/sebi-to-introduce-more-safeguards-for-f-o-as-traders-losses-mount-123012501318_1.html)

### Inferences
- In FY22, transaction costs alone turned a meaningful share of marginal traders from profit into loss. Profit-makers giving up 15-50% of their profits to costs means a high-turnover style is penalised even when the trader is right.

### Gaps
- No income profile or exact trading-frequency definition ("active" = ?) was found in the secondary sources. The SEBI PDF text could not be retrieved.

## 2. SEBI study of 23 Sep 2024 (FY22-FY24 data)

### Takeaway
Across FY22-FY24, 93% of more than 1 crore individual F&O traders lost money. The average loss was about Rs 2 lakh including costs, and aggregate losses exceeded Rs 1.8 lakh crore. In FY24 alone, proprietary traders and FPIs earned about Rs 61,000 crore before costs, and almost all of it came from algorithmic trading. Individuals lost about Rs 75,000 crore net in FY24.

### Cited Findings
- Title: "Study - Analysis of Profits & Losses in the Equity Derivatives Segment (FY22-FY24)". Published 23 Sep 2024 — [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/research/sep-2024/study-analysis-of-profits-and-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24-_86905.html)
- Loss-makers and aggregate losses — [Zerodha TradingQnA summary, 23 Sep 2024](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebis-latest-analysis-of-profits-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24/173596); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/fo-frenzy-over-93-retail-investors-lost-average-rs-2-lakh-in-three-years-says-sebi)
  - 93% of more than 1 crore individual traders made losses over FY22-FY24.
  - Average loss was about Rs 2 lakh per trader, including transaction costs.
  - Aggregate losses were above Rs 1.8 lakh crore.
- Top loss-makers: the worst 3.5% (about 4 lakh traders) lost an average of Rs 28 lakh each over the three years — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebis-latest-analysis-of-profits-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24/173596)
- Profitable traders: only about 1% of individuals earned more than Rs 1 lakh profit after costs. Another outlet says 7.2% made any profit over the three years — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebis-latest-analysis-of-profits-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24/173596); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/93-of-individual-traders-suffered-losses-in-fo-in-last-3-years-sebi-study)
- Transaction costs — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebis-latest-analysis-of-profits-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24/173596)
  - Individuals paid about Rs 50,000 crore in transaction costs over FY22-FY24.
  - In FY24 the average was about Rs 26,000 per trader.
  - Brokerage was 51% of costs and exchange fees 20%.
- FY24 gross P&L by investor category, before costs — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebis-latest-analysis-of-profits-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24/173596)
  - Proprietary traders made about Rs 33,000 crore.
  - FPIs made about Rs 28,000 crore.
  - DIIs made about Rs 200 crore.
  - Individuals lost about Rs 41,500 crore.
  - "Others" lost about Rs 19,700 crore.
  - Individuals and Others together lost more than Rs 61,000 crore.
- FY24 individual losses by instrument: individuals lost about Rs 55,000 crore in options and made about Rs 13,400 crore in futures — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebis-latest-analysis-of-profits-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24/173596)
- FY24 net loss for individuals, after costs:
  - Business Standard reports about Rs 75,000 crore — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/capital-market-news/sebi-study-exposes-massive-losses-for-individual-f-o-traders-in-india-124092400948_1.html)
  - SEBI's later July 2025 study gives Rs 74,812 crore, consistent with that — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/retail-f-o-losses-rose-to-over-1-lakh-crore-trader-participation-dropped-20-percent)
  - The ~Rs 61,000 crore figure is gross (before costs) and covers individuals plus others.
- Algorithmic trading — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebis-latest-analysis-of-profits-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24/173596)
  - Algo entities generated 97% of FPI profits and 96% of proprietary-trader profits.
  - 306 of 376 FPIs used algos (~81%), and 347 of 626 prop traders did (~55%).
  - About 13% of 95.7 lakh individual traders showed algo use. This is likely overstated because it includes broker auto square-offs.
- Age and income — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebis-latest-analysis-of-profits-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24/173596)
  - Traders under 30 were 31% in FY23 and 43% in FY24.
  - About 76% of traders in FY24 earned under Rs 5 lakh a year, up from 71% in FY22.
  - Traders beyond the top 30 cities (B30) made up 72% of the F&O base.
- Persistence — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebis-latest-analysis-of-profits-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24/173596)
  - 76.3% of traders who lost money in both FY22 and FY23 kept trading in FY24.
  - Of the 24.4 lakh traders who lost in both years, 18.6 lakh continued.
  - Only 8.3% of those who continued made a profit in FY24.

### Inferences
- The FY24 split is the core design fact: options were where individuals lost (about Rs 55k crore), while futures were marginally profitable for them. The counterparties were largely algorithmic prop traders and FPIs.

### Gaps
- No primary-source figure was found for loss-making share among frequent versus occasional traders in the Sep 2024 study, nor for transaction costs as a percentage of losses in that study. A claim of "96% for traders with more than 500 trades" could not be verified.

## 3. 2025-2026 SEBI updates: did participation and losses change after the November 2024 curbs?

### Takeaway
FY25 (curbs started mid-year) was the worst year for losses: Rs 1.06-1.12 lakh crore, with about 91% of traders losing money. In FY26, the first full year under the curbs, the number of traders fell about 18-20% and aggregate losses fell to Rs 91,685 crore, with 87.7% losing money. However, the average loss per remaining trader rose slightly, to about Rs 1.17 lakh.

### Cited Findings
- July 2025 study, reported 7 Jul 2025 — [The Week/PTI, 7 Jul 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/07/07/dcm92-biz-sebi-derivatives-study.html); [Angel One, 9 Jul 2025](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/retail-f-o-losses-rose-to-over-1-lakh-crore-trader-participation-dropped-20-percent)
  - About 91% of individuals lost money in FY25.
  - Net losses were Rs 1,05,603 crore in FY25, against Rs 74,812 crore in FY24 (up 41%).
  - Average loss was about Rs 1.1 lakh in FY25, against Rs 86,728 in FY24.
  - Unique individual traders were down 20% year on year but still 24% above FY23.
  - Losses rose through Q1-Q3 of FY25 and fell in Q4.
  - The sample was the top 13 brokers, about 96 lakh traders.
- Net losses in earlier years, as given in the July 2025 study: FY22 Rs 40,824 crore and FY23 Rs 65,747 crore. The cumulative FY22-FY25 total was Rs 2,86,986 crore — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/retail-f-o-losses-rose-to-over-1-lakh-crore-trader-participation-dropped-20-percent)
- Activity from Dec 2024 to May 2025, after the curbs, compared with a year earlier — [The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/07/07/dcm92-biz-sebi-derivatives-study.html)
  - Index options turnover fell 9% in premium terms and 29% in notional terms.
  - Individuals' premium turnover fell 11%, but was 36% higher than two years earlier.
- August 2026 studies (FY25-FY26), from the Department of Economic and Policy Analysis (DEPA). Titles: "Profitability of Individual Traders in the Equity Derivatives Segment (FY25-FY26)" and "Trading Behaviour of Individual Traders in the Equity Derivatives Segment (FY25-FY26)" — [SEBI profitability study](https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/research/aug-2026/study-profitability-of-individual-traders-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy25-fy26-_103835.html); [SEBI behaviour study](https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/research/aug-2026/study-trading-behaviour-of-individual-traders-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy25-fy26-_103836.html); summarised by [The Policy Edge, 20 Aug 2026](https://www.policyedge.in/p/nearly-9-in-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-as-participation-fell-18-sebi-studies)
  - Active traders fell from 106.2 lakh in FY25 to 87.5 lakh in FY26 (-18%).
  - The share losing money fell from 90.9% to 87.7%.
  - Net losses fell from Rs 1.12 lakh crore (revised FY25) to Rs 91,685 crore (-18%).
  - Average net loss rose from Rs 1.14 lakh to Rs 1.17 lakh (+2.4%).
  - Options accounted for 92% of aggregate individual losses.
- Costs in FY26 — [The Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/nearly-9-in-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-as-participation-fell-18-sebi-studies)
  - Individuals paid Rs 24,859 crore in transaction costs, of which STT was Rs 6,645 crore (27%).
  - About 4.4 lakh traders who were profitable before costs became net loss-makers after costs.
- Experience does not help — [The Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/nearly-9-in-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-as-participation-fell-18-sebi-studies); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/sebi-futures-options-study-retail-traders-trading-losses-experience)
  - About 91% of first-year traders lost money, rising to more than 95% of those active four or more consecutive years.
  - 90-92% of traders who lost in two consecutive years lost again in the next.
  - Among traders active in all five years from FY22 to FY26, 65.6% lost money every year and 0.5% were profitable every year.
  - About 88% of traders with cumulative losses above Rs 10 lakh kept trading.
- Frequency, from the behavioural sample — [The Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/nearly-9-in-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-as-participation-fell-18-sebi-studies)
  - Traders active more than 100 days were 42% of traders, but accounted for 94% of turnover and 87% of losses.
  - Caveat: the behavioural findings come from a random sample of 5,050 traders and are "indicative".
- Capital size — [The Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/nearly-9-in-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-as-participation-fell-18-sebi-studies)
  - 77% of traders had peak margin below Rs 1 lakh, and about 90% of them lost money.
  - The 23% with peak margin above Rs 1 lakh accounted for 92% of turnover and 86% of losses.
  - About 77% of the roughly 1.10 crore traders who lost money in FY22-FY24 held equity portfolios in FY26 worth less than a quarter of their cumulative losses.
- Age and income in FY26 — [Outlook Business/PTI, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/derivatives-market-gets-gen-z-twist-but-losses-cast-a-long-shadow-sebi)
  - Traders under 30 were 43% of participants (31% four years earlier), and about 89% of them lost money.
  - About 81% of traders over 60 lost money.
  - About three-quarters of traders earned under Rs 5 lakh a year. This group generated 43% of turnover and 53% of losses, with an 88% loss rate. The loss rate for those earning above Rs 1 crore was 81%.
  - About two-thirds of traders came from B30 cities.
- Parliamentary reply, Rajya Sabha (MoS Finance Pankaj Chaudhary), reported 11 Aug 2026 — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/sebi-measures-reduce-equity-fo-losses-for-retail-investors-in-fy26)
  - Unique individual investors fell from 98.10 lakh in FY25 to 78.60 lakh in FY26.
  - Individual net losses were Rs 1,11,788 crore in FY25 and Rs 91,685 crore in FY26.
  - Average loss was Rs 1,13,913 in FY25 and Rs 1,16,654 in FY26.
  - Equity derivatives turnover fell from Rs 213 lakh crore to Rs 202 lakh crore.
- SEBI annual report: reportedly a 51.5% fall in total options contract volumes in FY26 compared with FY25 — [Outlook Money (search snippet)](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/sebi-annual-report-know-what-changed-from-your-social-media-feed-to-your-portfolio-in-fy26)

### Inferences
- The curbs reduced how many people traded and the total amount lost, but not how much each remaining trader lost or how likely they were to lose. A tool cannot assume the regulatory changes made retail options buying a fair game.

### Gaps
- Trader counts differ by source: 106.2 lakh vs 87.5 lakh (study sample base) against 98.1 lakh vs 78.6 lakh (parliamentary reply). This probably reflects different definitions (active traders vs unique investors), but no source confirms that.
- The FY25 loss figure was revised from Rs 1,05,603 crore (July 2025) to about Rs 1,11,788-1.12 lakh crore (2026).
- No prop or FPI profit figures were found for FY25 or FY26.

## 4. Option buyers vs sellers, intraday vs positional, and expiry-day concentration

### Takeaway
SEBI's FY26 behavioural study shows retail is overwhelmingly on the buy side: 97% are predominantly options buyers. Sellers lose less often (about 44%) but lose large amounts when they do. Options, not futures, account for the bulk of retail losses (92% in FY26, and about Rs 55k crore of options losses against a futures profit in FY24).

### Cited Findings
- 97% of the behavioural sample were predominantly options buyers, and 93% bought options exclusively — [The Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/nearly-9-in-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-as-participation-fell-18-sebi-studies)
- Options sellers had a loss incidence of about 44%. Losing sellers lost about Rs 51.7 lakh each on average in FY26 — [The Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/nearly-9-in-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-as-participation-fell-18-sebi-studies)
- Options made up 92% of aggregate individual losses in FY26 — [The Policy Edge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/nearly-9-in-10-individual-fo-traders-lost-money-as-participation-fell-18-sebi-studies)
- In FY24, individuals lost about Rs 55,000 crore in options and made about Rs 13,400 crore in futures — [TradingQnA](https://tradingqna.com/t/sebis-latest-analysis-of-profits-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24/173596)
- The 1 Oct 2024 measures were aimed specifically at the "high-risk environment of index options trading on expiry days" — [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/sebi-tightens-regulations-on-equity-derivatives-trading/75288.html)

### Inferences
- Since most retail traders are buyers and options drive the losses, a buying tool has to assume a high base rate of loss. Small, defined-risk positions and low trade frequency are the variables the evidence supports: frequent traders and higher-turnover traders carry most of the losses.

### Gaps
- No SEBI or NSE figure from this research was found for intraday vs positional P&L, or for the share of retail options turnover traded on expiry day. The Policy Edge notes the FY26 studies do not quantify expiry-day activity.

## 5. SEBI circular of 1 Oct 2024: measures and dates

### Takeaway
Circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD-1/P/CIR/2024/132 (1 Oct 2024) phased in six measures between 20 Nov 2024 and 1 Apr 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Circular: "Measures to strengthen Equity Index Derivatives framework for increased investor protection and market stability", 1 Oct 2024 — [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2024/measures-to-strengthen-equity-index-derivatives-framework-for-increased-investor-protection-and-market-stability_87208.html); [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/10/03/measures-to-strengthen-equity-index-derivatives-framework-legal-news)
- Effective dates — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/business-updates/sebis-new-rules-for-index-derivatives-heres-whats-changing); [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/sebi-tightens-regulations-on-equity-derivatives-trading/75288.html); [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/10/03/measures-to-strengthen-equity-index-derivatives-framework-legal-news)

| Measure | Effective date | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| One weekly expiry per exchange | 20 Nov 2024 | NSE kept only Nifty 50 weekly. Bank Nifty, FinNifty, Midcap Select and Next 50 weeklies were discontinued. BSE kept only Sensex. |
| Additional 2% ELM on short options on expiry day (higher tail-risk coverage) | 20 Nov 2024 | 2% of notional on short options expiring that day. |
| Minimum contract size raised | New contracts from 20/21 Nov 2024 (sources differ by a day) | Minimum contract value moved from Rs 5-10 lakh to at least Rs 15 lakh (Rs 15-20 lakh band). Nifty lot went from 25 to 75; first new-lot weekly was 2 Jan 2025. Sensex lot went from 10 to 20. |
| Upfront collection of option premium from buyers | 1 Feb 2025 | Brokers must collect the premium upfront. |
| Calendar-spread margin benefit removed on expiry day | 1 Feb 2025 per the circular/SCC. Zerodha states 10 Feb 2025. | Flag: small discrepancy. |
| Intraday monitoring of position limits | 1 Apr 2025 | At least 4 random snapshots a day, instead of end-of-day only. |

### Inferences
- For a retail buyer, the upfront-premium and spread rules change little. The binding constraints are lot size (bigger minimum ticket) and the loss of Bank Nifty and other weekly expiries, which leaves only Nifty (NSE) and Sensex (BSE) weeklies.

### Gaps
- The 1 Apr 2025 date for intraday monitoring rests on Zerodha's write-up. The circular text itself was not retrieved.

## 6. 2025-2026 changes: Tuesday expiry, lot sizes, FutEq OI, pre-open, STT

### Takeaway
2025 brought:
- a new open-interest metric (FutEq) and position-limit framework (circular of 29 May 2025),
- NSE expiries moving to Tuesday (from 1 Sep 2025),
- intraday limits on index options positions (1 Sep 2025 circular),
- a pre-open session for F&O (8 Dec 2025),
- smaller index lots (Nifty 65, Bank Nifty 30, from late Dec 2025 to Jan 2026).

On 1 Apr 2026 the Union Budget raised STT on options premium from 0.1% to 0.15%.

### Cited Findings
- Circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD-1/P/CIR/2025/79 of 29 May 2025, "Measures for Enhancing Trading Convenience and Strengthening Risk Monitoring in Equity Derivatives" — [BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/sebi-equity-fo-rules-futeq-method-mwpl-risk-management-478321-2025-05-29); [Business Standard (snippet)](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/sebi-fo-revamp-new-oi-metric-position-limits-july-december-rollout-125052901798_1.html)
  - Open interest is now measured as futures-equivalent (delta-based) OI.
  - End-of-day limits for index options are Rs 1,500 crore net FutEq and Rs 10,000 crore gross per entity.
  - Rollout was phased from July to December 2025.
  - The new MWPL (market-wide position limit, which triggers stock F&O bans) is the lower of 15% of free float or 65 times average daily delivery value. It took effect on 1 Oct 2025 — [NSE Clearing circular (via broker)](https://www.ricago.com/assets/front/base/file/file_management/3776.pdf)
  - Intraday MWPL monitoring (4 random snapshots a day) began on 3 Nov 2025 — [NSE Clearing circular (via broker)](https://www.ricago.com/assets/front/base/file/file_management/3776.pdf)
  - Non-benchmark indices must have at least 14 constituents, with weight caps on the top ones, to be eligible for derivatives — [search summary of BusinessToday/Bajaj Broking](https://www.bajajbroking.in/blog/sebi-notifies-new-f-and-o-rules-with-higher-index-limits-and-risk-measures)
- Intraday index-options limits (circular of 1 Sep 2025): net intraday FutEq position capped at Rs 5,000 crore per entity — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/equity/sebi-outlines-new-framework-for-monitoring-of-intraday-position-limits-for-index-derivatives); [Structured Retail Products](https://www.structuredretailproducts.com/insights/81193/indian-regulator-tightens-rules-on-intraday-position-limits-in-index-options1)
- Expiry day — [IANS](https://ianslive.in/nse-likely-gets-sebis-nod-to-shift-derivatives-expiry-to-tuesday--20250617164213); [All India Radio, 1 Sep 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/nse-bse-swap-derivatives-expiry-days); [Business Today, 18 Jun 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/nse-bse-fo-expiry-days-what-has-changed-for-stock-investors-history-more-480781-2025-06-18)
  - SEBI approved the change in mid-June 2025.
  - NSE contracts expiring on or after 1 Sep 2025 expire on Tuesday (Nifty weekly and all NSE monthlies). BSE (Sensex) moved to Thursday.
  - Exchanges are limited to Tuesday or Thursday expiries.
- Lot-size revision (NSE circular of 3 Oct 2025) — [Zerodha bulletin, 4 Oct 2025](https://zerodha.com/marketintel/bulletin/429705/revision-in-lot-size-of-index-derivative-contracts-from-december-30-2025); [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/bulletins/lot-size-changes-in-index-derivatives-effective-december-30-2025/)
  - New lots: Nifty 75 to 65, Bank Nifty 35 to 30, FinNifty 65 to 60, Midcap Select 140 to 120. Next 50 stays at 25.
  - Nifty weeklies use the new lot from the 6 Jan 2026 expiry.
  - Monthlies use it from the 27 Jan 2026 expiry.
  - Quarterly and half-yearly contracts switch at end of day on 30 Dec 2025.
  - Search summaries give Bank Nifty's previous lot as 35 (it had moved from 30 to 35 during 2025), but no primary source was found for that interim change.
- Pre-open session in equity derivatives started 8 Dec 2025 according to exchange and broker notices. Some press reports say 6 Dec — [ICICI Direct](https://www.icicidirect.com/futures-and-options/articles/pre-open-session-introduced-in-equity-derivatives-f-o-segment); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/announcements/market/pre-open-session-introduced-in-equity-derivatives-fo)
- STT increase announced in Union Budget 2026, effective for trades from 1 Apr 2026 — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/stt-hike-from-april-1-2026-budget-what-it-means-for-futures-and-options-traders); [5paisa](https://www.5paisa.com/news/stt-hike-on-fo-to-take-effect-from-april-1-amid-rising-options-activity)
  - Futures: 0.02% to 0.05%.

### Inferences
- FutEq limits, MWPL and the intraday limits bind large entities, not a trader with Rs 10k-25k. Their retail-visible effect is fewer and shorter stock F&O bans, because MWPL is now measured on delta. Ban lists still matter: a trader cannot open new positions in a banned stock.

### Gaps
- No primary 2026 SEBI circular introducing new retail F&O restrictions was found. Blog claims of a "50:50 margin rule" or further 2026 lot changes were unverified and are excluded.
- Changes to stock F&O eligibility criteria in 2025-26 were not confirmed. NSE's selection-criteria page exists ([NSE](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-selection-criteria)) but was not read.

It may cover all investors and a different period. Treat it as unverified.

## 7. What it means for a retail buyer of NIFTY/BANKNIFTY/stock options with Rs 10k-25k per trade

### Takeaway
A Rs 10k-25k budget buys roughly one to a few lots of a Nifty weekly option at typical out-of-the-money or at-the-money premiums. Bank Nifty has no weekly expiry, so its options cost more per lot. The evidence says to trade rarely, keep risk defined, and treat costs as a first-order drag.

### Cited Findings (rules that bind)
- Nifty lot is 65 from Jan 2026. Bank Nifty lot is 30 and monthly only, expiring on the last Tuesday. Nifty weekly expires on Tuesday — [Zerodha bulletin](https://zerodha.com/marketintel/bulletin/429705/revision-in-lot-size-of-index-derivative-contracts-from-december-30-2025); [All India Radio](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/nse-bse-swap-derivatives-expiry-days); [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/business-updates/sebis-new-rules-for-index-derivatives-heres-whats-changing)
- The full premium must be collected upfront. A buyer cannot use leverage on the premium — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/10/03/measures-to-strengthen-equity-index-derivatives-framework-legal-news)
- STT on the options premium is 0.15% on the sell side from 1 Apr 2026, and 0.15% of intrinsic value if an option is exercised in the money — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/stt-hike-from-april-1-2026-budget-what-it-means-for-futures-and-options-traders)
- Stock options: when a stock enters the MWPL ban, no new positions are allowed. The ban methodology changed on 1 Oct 2025 — [NSE Clearing circular (via broker)](https://www.ricago.com/assets/front/base/file/file_management/3776.pdf)

### Inferences
- Simple arithmetic, not sourced: a Nifty premium of Rs 150 × 65 = Rs 9,750 per lot, so Rs 10k-25k is 1-2 lots at that premium. A Bank Nifty monthly premium of Rs 500 × 30 = Rs 15,000 per lot. The sizing logic should compute lot cost = premium × current lot size from exchange contract specifications rather than hard-code it, because lots are revised twice a year.
- Exercised in-the-money options attract STT on intrinsic value, so the tool should square off before expiry rather than let options exercise.
- Evidence-based guardrails:
  - Limit trading days: traders active more than 100 days generated 87% of losses.
  - Small accounts are not safer: about 90% of traders with peak margin under Rs 1 lakh lost money.
  - Stop after repeated losses: 90-92% of two-year losers lose again.
  - Track all-in costs: 4.4 lakh traders were profitable before costs but lost money after them in FY26.

### Gaps
- No SEBI data was found on outcomes specifically for small-ticket (Rs 10k-25k) option buyers, or on Nifty versus Bank Nifty P&L.
