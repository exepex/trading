## How to read this

- **Bias** blends trend, momentum, RSI, put/call OI ratio and position between OI walls. It is a weighted heuristic, not a prediction.
- **Chance of profit** uses the option market's own implied volatility. A setup can be worth taking below 50% if the payoff is asymmetric.
- Prices assume you buy at the ask and sell at the bid. Real fills may be better or worse.
- Log a setup with `python -m fno paper open <id>` to track it. You place real orders yourself on Groww.
