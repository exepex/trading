Backtest over the last 30 days of 5-minute prices

APPROXIMATION: option prices are modelled (Black-Scholes, volatility from the stock's own 5-minute moves x 1.1, floor 8%, about 10 days to expiry), not real quotes; no bid-ask spread beyond charges; only rules that can be judged from price history vote (no option chain, OI, news or research). Each name is simulated on its own: the re-entry and post-loss pauses apply, but not the portfolio limits (open trades, trades per day, families), the shortlist, the contract picker or the move check. Holidays are not known, so a holiday counts as a holding day. Rule votes weaker than 0.2 are not graded. Use it to compare rules, not to predict profit.
