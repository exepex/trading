Jargon buster

Call optionGains when the index rises. You pay a premium for it.
Put optionGains when the index falls. You pay a premium for it.
StrikeThe index level the option is based on, such as 22,500.
ExpiryThe date the option ends. NSE index options expire on Tuesdays.
LotThe fixed unit you trade in. One NIFTY lot is 65 units.
SpreadBuying one option and selling another to cut the cost and cap the risk.
Open interest (OI)How many contracts are still open at a strike. Big piles show where traders expect a floor or ceiling.
Put/call ratioOpen puts divided by open calls. Above 1 leans positive, below 1 leans negative.
Implied volatilityHow big a move option prices expect. High IV means options are expensive.
Max painThe level where option buyers as a group would lose the most at expiry.
Stop lossThe loss at which you exit, no matter what, so one bad trade stays small.
Paper tradeA practice trade tracked as if real, with no money at risk.
Research aid for a paper-trading experiment, not investment advice. Direction comes from a weighted mix of trend, momentum, RSI, put/call ratio and open-interest levels; it can be wrong.
